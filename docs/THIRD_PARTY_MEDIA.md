# Third-party media and attribution

The community repository includes a small set of Athan notification recordings
because their source pages and redistribution terms are recorded in
`assets/audio/notifications/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.

Those recordings are not relicensed under Apache-2.0. Keep each file's listed
Creative Commons or public-domain terms, required credit, and share-alike
requirements when redistributing them.

The previous private development checkout also contained local nasheed files,
generated marketing media, screenshots, and work-in-progress audio. Those are
not part of this community snapshot until ownership or redistribution rights
are documented. Please do not re-add them without a source and license review.
