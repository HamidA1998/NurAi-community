# Code of Conduct

NurAI is a community for building respectful, useful software for Muslims and
the people who support them. Participants must help keep discussions safe and
constructive.

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful of people, cultures, languages, schools of thought, abilities,
  genders, backgrounds, and levels of technical or religious knowledge.
- Critique ideas and code without insults, harassment, threats, dogpiling, or
  personal attacks.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish personal data, private conversations,
  account details, health information, or credentials.
- Be honest about sources, testing, authorship, licensing, and uncertainty.
- Keep feedback relevant to the work and make room for quieter contributors.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, sexualised or
violent abuse, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private information, deliberate
deception, spam, and attempts to bypass project safety or access controls.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through GitHub to the repository owner
[@HamidA1998](https://github.com/HamidA1998). Do not open a public issue for
private safety reports. Reports will be reviewed in confidence where possible.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
