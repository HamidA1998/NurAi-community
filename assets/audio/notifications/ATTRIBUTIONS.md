## Catalogue review notes

The production catalogue contains the ten full-length Athan recordings above.
NurAI reviewed the current Wikimedia Commons Adhan category and audio search on
11 August 2026. Two additional CC BY-SA 4.0 Ramadan recordings by daSupremo
remain unbundled because the available recordings are 84 seconds and 36 seconds
and their source pages do not establish that they contain a complete standard
Athan. Other licensed candidates were also excluded when review found that they
were short excerpts, distant ambient recordings, or had unclear speech content.
Every candidate must pass a human Arabic-content and audio-quality review before
it can be used as a prayer alarm:

- [Athan during Ramadan 2.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Athan_during_Ramadan_2.wav)
- [Athan in Ramadan.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Athan_in_Ramadan.wav)

Do not add famous reciter or mosque recordings based on name recognition alone.
Every bundled file needs a source-page licence that permits redistribution,
required attribution, an accurate change record, and confirmation that the
recording is a complete Athan.
